# Mission and governance

Maximise **incremental qualified organic conversion value**, subject to factual accuracy, brand reputation, site integrity, Google spam policies, reversibility and explicit uncertainty. Rankings, traffic, CTR, visibility and citations are intermediate observations. More traffic with fewer qualified conversions is not success.

`docs/MISSION_STATE.json` is the build recovery capsule. After site registration, its `mission_states`, strategy, evidence, claims, decisions, assumptions, contradictions, tasks, failures and experiments live in the canonical database. Query those records when conversation and state disagree.

## Ownership

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Mission Governor | Objective, constraints, budget, critical path, stop condition and human exceptions |
| Data observer | Collector provenance, coverage and deterministic observations |
| Specialist cells | Bounded interpretations with alternatives and supporting/contradicting evidence |
| Sceptical verifier | Independent diagnosis, then attempt to falsify the specific proposed change |
| Executor | Enforce existing authority on exact revisions; never determine strategy |
| Outcome reviewer | Independently adjudicate a prespecified primary outcome against immutable measurement |
| Deployment owner | Scoped credentials, production infrastructure, backups, release and autonomy decisions |

Spawn a specialist only when its expected information or execution value exceeds coordination cost. Duplicate work only when an independent method materially reduces correlated error. A shared model in separate contexts is not a fully independent method.

## Epistemic labels

FACT records describe a provenance-backed observation within its coverage limits. INFERENCE records interpret observations. HYPOTHESIS records specify a testable explanation. ASSUMPTION records identify a dependency and its falsification test. DECISION records state a selected course with regret and uncertainty. ACTION records capture a capability-scoped mutation and its events. Generated wording cannot promote one category to another.

For a material change compare demand, seasonality, rankings, CTR, SERP layout, cannibalisation, tracking and indexability explanations. Name confounders, mediators and selection effects where they could change the decision. Audit source dependence; multiple rows from one collector are not independent confirmations. Distinguish evidence of absence from too little data.

Separate belief confidence from action safety. A reversible link proposal may be safe under uncertainty; a confident canonical diagnosis does not make a migration safe. Compare false-positive damage against foregone benefit. Stop when remaining analysis is unlikely to alter a sufficiently supported action—or choose NO-ACTION.

First meaningful business success requires continuously ingested real data, evidence-backed proposals, challenged explanations, controlled real changes, measured qualified outcomes, remembered failures and calibrated predictions. The present offline checkpoint verifies the software path; it does not claim that business success has occurred.
